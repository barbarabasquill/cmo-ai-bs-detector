05 / THE CMO RESPONSE

A red flag is a reason to investigate—not a conviction.

Do not accuse the writer, agency, or AI. Point to the exact weakness, request the missing evidence, make the repair, and keep a human accountable for the final decision.

  1. STOP Pause publication or approval when the risk is material.
  2. ASK Use the exact questions from the action card.
  3. VERIFY Confirm claims with an owner, primary source, or customer evidence.
  4. REPAIR Use the recommended prompt and review the revised work.
