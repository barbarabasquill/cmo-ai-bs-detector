## AI sounds confident. That does not mean it is right.

AI can produce a campaign strategy in 30 seconds. It can also invent the research, flatten the customer into a fictional persona, repeat an executive’s assumptions, and package the whole mess in flawless corporate prose.

**The CMO’s AI BS Detector** helps marketing leaders decide whether an AI-generated deliverable is useful, questionable, or weapons-grade nonsense—before a customer, salesperson, board member, or legal team does it for them.

Use it to review:

- Campaign and go-to-market plans
- Messaging and positioning
- Personas and ICP summaries
- Executive thought leadership
- Demand-generation emails
- ABM plays and sales enablement
- Performance reports and board summaries

> **The point is not to catch AI using AI. The point is to make human judgment harder to skip.**

## How the detector works

Paste AI-generated marketing copy into the browser-based tool and select **Analyze the BS**. The detector looks for risk signals across six dimensions and returns a score, a verdict, the problems it found, and recommended next actions.

| Test | Weight | What it looks for |
|---|---:|---|
| **Evidence and sourcing** | 25 | Unsupported statistics, unnamed studies, invented quotes, and claims presented as fact |
| **Specificity** | 20 | Generic buyers, vague outcomes, interchangeable language, and context-free recommendations |
| **Strategic substance** | 20 | Lists of tactics pretending to be strategy; no choices, tradeoffs, sequencing, or ownership |
| **Brand voice** | 15 | Robotic phrasing, cliché density, excessive adjectives, and copy any competitor could use |
| **Original insight** | 10 | Restatement of the prompt, obvious observations, and conclusions with no useful point of view |
| **Risk and compliance** | 10 | Privacy, legal, bias, disclosure, brand, and reputational warning signs |

### The four verdicts

| Score | Verdict | What it means |
|---:|---|---|
| **85–100** | **Surprisingly Human** | Specific, credible, useful, and ready for final human approval |
| **70–84** | **Useful Frenemy** | Promising, but facts, differentiation, or assumptions need checking |
| **50–69** | **Corporate Oatmeal** | Polished words with very little nutritional value |
| **0–49** | **Weapons-Grade BS** | Unsupported, generic, risky, or confidently wrong |

## What it can catch

- Empty phrases such as “in today’s rapidly evolving landscape”
- Overuse of “unlock,” “leverage,” “revolutionize,” and “game-changing”
- Statistics and ROI claims that have no named source
- Absolutes such as “always,” “never,” “guaranteed,” and “everyone”
- Suspicious quotation or research patterns
- Long passages with no concrete example, buyer, product, or outcome
- Personas built without customer evidence
- Tactics presented as a strategy
- Recommendations that ignore constraints, tradeoffs, or ownership
- Copy that flatters the premise instead of challenging it

## What it cannot do

This is a **risk detector**, not a factual verification engine. It can flag a claim that needs evidence; it cannot prove every claim true or false. It does not replace customer research, source checking, brand review, legal review, privacy review, or executive accountability.

**A high score means “reviewed against these signals.” It does not mean “automatically safe to publish.”**

## Privacy by design

The interactive detector runs entirely in the visitor’s browser.

- No sign-in
- No API key
- No database
- No visitor text sent to the repository owner
- No customer content stored
- No AI vendor connection
- No installation required

> **Privacy:** Your text is analyzed locally in your browser. This tool does not transmit, retain, or train on your content.

Do not paste restricted, regulated, confidential, customer, employee, legal, security, or unreleased financial information into any unapproved tool. Follow your company’s data-handling and AI-use policies.

## Start in two minutes

1. Open the [interactive detector](https://barbarabasquill.github.io/cmo-ai-bs-detector/).

## For repository builders

This project is intentionally simple: plain HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Markdown. There is no build step, backend, database, package installation, or API dependency.

To publish your own version:

1. Fork or clone the repository.
2. Keep `index.html`, `styles.css`, and `app.js` in the repository root.
3. In GitHub, open **Settings → Pages**.
4. Under **Build and deployment**, choose **Deploy from a branch**.
5. Select `main` and `/ (root)`, then save.
6. Test the live site on desktop and mobile.

## Responsible use

- Use the detector to create review questions, not automatic accusations.
- Verify claims against primary, authoritative sources.
- Never use an AI-generated persona as a substitute for customer research.
- Do not publish AI output without a named human owner.
- Escalate regulated, legal, privacy, security, or reputational questions to the appropriate expert.
- Document material AI use when your organization or audience requires disclosure.

Read the complete [methodology](docs/methodology.md), [limitations](docs/limitations.md), [privacy statement](docs/privacy.md), and [responsible AI disclosure](docs/responsible-ai-disclosure.md).
