# CMO Board Narrative

## Outcome

Turn marketing performance into a concise board story about growth, risk, decisions, and next actions.

## Start with

- company goals, marketing results, pipeline, risks, changes, decisions needed

If a required input is unavailable, label it **Unknown** or **Assumption**. Do not fill gaps with plausible fiction.

## Easy-Peezy workflow

1. **Frame:** Lead with business outcome
2. **Ground:** separate signal from activity
3. **Decide:** explain variance
4. **Build:** connect programs to evidence
5. **Control:** state risks and confidence
6. **Learn:** make explicit decision requests

## Deliver

- board storyline, slide outline, metric commentary, risk table, decision slide

Lead with the recommendation. Keep the main response to one executive page when practical; place working detail after it. End with **Decide now / Do next / Verify before use**.

## Human approval gate

Do not cherry-pick windows, mix sourced and influenced revenue, or hide data-quality limitations.

External publishing, sending, spending, deleting, or system changes require explicit authorization.

## Score it with

- revenue quality, pipeline coverage, efficiency, forecast confidence, decision closure

## Quality bar

- Tie every recommendation to a customer, commercial, category, or capability decision.
- Separate verified facts, reasonable inferences, and open assumptions.
- Remove corporate oatmeal and unsupported precision.
- Name an owner, a next action, and the evidence that would change the decision.
