# CMO AI BS Audit

## Outcome

Review AI-generated marketing for unsupported claims, corporate oatmeal, fake insight, yes-man behavior, and risk.

## Start with

- original prompt, AI output, source material, intended audience, approval standard

If a required input is unavailable, label it **Unknown** or **Assumption**. Do not fill gaps with plausible fiction.

## Easy-Peezy workflow

1. **Frame:** Separate claims and opinions
2. **Ground:** test evidence
3. **Decide:** score specificity and strategic substance
4. **Build:** detect generic phrasing and false agreement
5. **Control:** identify risk
6. **Learn:** produce a repair brief

## Deliver

- BS score, verdict, flagged claims, repair instructions, human-approval checklist

Lead with the recommendation. Keep the main response to one executive page when practical; place working detail after it. End with **Decide now / Do next / Verify before use**.

## Human approval gate

This is a risk detector, not a truth engine. Every consequential claim still requires source verification.

External publishing, sending, spending, deleting, or system changes require explicit authorization.

## Score it with

- unsupported claims removed, specificity, approval cycle time, incidents avoided, output usefulness

## Quality bar

- Tie every recommendation to a customer, commercial, category, or capability decision.
- Separate verified facts, reasonable inferences, and open assumptions.
- Remove corporate oatmeal and unsupported precision.
- Name an owner, a next action, and the evidence that would change the decision.
