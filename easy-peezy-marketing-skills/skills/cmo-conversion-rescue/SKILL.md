# CMO Conversion Rescue

## Outcome

Diagnose why a page or form is losing qualified buyers and prioritize fixes by evidence and impact.

## Start with

- page, traffic source, audience, conversion event, behavior data, constraints

If a required input is unavailable, label it **Unknown** or **Assumption**. Do not fill gaps with plausible fiction.

## Easy-Peezy workflow

1. **Frame:** Check traffic-message match
2. **Ground:** inspect clarity and proof
3. **Decide:** audit friction and anxiety
4. **Build:** segment leaks
5. **Control:** rank fixes
6. **Learn:** define experiments and no-test improvements

## Deliver

- diagnostic, prioritized fixes, experiment backlog, wireframe notes, measurement plan

Lead with the recommendation. Keep the main response to one executive page when practical; place working detail after it. End with **Decide now / Do next / Verify before use**.

## Human approval gate

Do not recommend manipulative urgency, hidden consent, or removing necessary qualification merely to inflate conversion.

External publishing, sending, spending, deleting, or system changes require explicit authorization.

## Score it with

- qualified conversion, completion rate, opportunity rate, revenue per visitor, error rate

## Quality bar

- Tie every recommendation to a customer, commercial, category, or capability decision.
- Separate verified facts, reasonable inferences, and open assumptions.
- Remove corporate oatmeal and unsupported precision.
- Name an owner, a next action, and the evidence that would change the decision.
