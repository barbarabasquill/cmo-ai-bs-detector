# CMO Customer Expansion

## Outcome

Design customer marketing that increases adoption, advocacy, renewal confidence, and expansion without treating customers as leads.

## Start with

- customer segments, lifecycle, usage, outcomes, renewal timing, account ownership

If a required input is unavailable, label it **Unknown** or **Assumption**. Do not fill gaps with plausible fiction.

## Easy-Peezy workflow

1. **Frame:** Identify customer value state
2. **Ground:** segment moments and needs
3. **Decide:** reinforce outcomes
4. **Build:** coordinate education, advocacy, and expansion
5. **Control:** align CSM and sales roles
6. **Learn:** measure health and revenue responsibly

## Deliver

- customer journey, program portfolio, message map, owner model, expansion scorecard

Lead with the recommendation. Keep the main response to one executive page when practical; place working detail after it. End with **Decide now / Do next / Verify before use**.

## Human approval gate

Do not exploit health scores or surprise customers with sales motions. Protect consent and relationship context.

External publishing, sending, spending, deleting, or system changes require explicit authorization.

## Score it with

- adoption, advocacy, renewal, expansion, customer sentiment

## Quality bar

- Tie every recommendation to a customer, commercial, category, or capability decision.
- Separate verified facts, reasonable inferences, and open assumptions.
- Remove corporate oatmeal and unsupported precision.
- Name an owner, a next action, and the evidence that would change the decision.
