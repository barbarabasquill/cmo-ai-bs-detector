# CMO Competitor Dossier

## Outcome

Build a sourced competitor profile focused on choices, proof, buyer implications, and likely moves.

## Start with

- competitor URLs, category, buyer, win-loss input, timeframe

If a required input is unavailable, label it **Unknown** or **Assumption**. Do not fill gaps with plausible fiction.

## Easy-Peezy workflow

1. **Frame:** Separate facts from inference
2. **Ground:** map positioning, offers, proof, channels, and product signals
3. **Decide:** identify strengths and vulnerabilities
4. **Build:** state likely response
5. **Control:** propose decisions

## Deliver

- sourced dossier, comparison table, implication memo, monitoring questions

Lead with the recommendation. Keep the main response to one executive page when practical; place working detail after it. End with **Decide now / Do next / Verify before use**.

## Human approval gate

Use public or authorized information only. Cite time-sensitive claims and label inference clearly.

External publishing, sending, spending, deleting, or system changes require explicit authorization.

## Score it with

- win rate, competitive mentions, response time, evidence freshness, field adoption

## Quality bar

- Tie every recommendation to a customer, commercial, category, or capability decision.
- Separate verified facts, reasonable inferences, and open assumptions.
- Remove corporate oatmeal and unsupported precision.
- Name an owner, a next action, and the evidence that would change the decision.
