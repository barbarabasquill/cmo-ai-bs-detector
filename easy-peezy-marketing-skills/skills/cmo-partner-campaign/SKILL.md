# CMO Partner Campaign

## Outcome

Turn a possible partnership into a balanced campaign with shared audience, value, work, and measurement.

## Start with

- partner fit, audience overlap, mutual value, channel access, commitments

If a required input is unavailable, label it **Unknown** or **Assumption**. Do not fill gaps with plausible fiction.

## Easy-Peezy workflow

1. **Frame:** Score fit
2. **Ground:** define the shared promise
3. **Decide:** choose a simple asset and activation
4. **Build:** divide responsibilities
5. **Control:** agree lead handling and brand approval
6. **Learn:** set reciprocal metrics

## Deliver

- partner scorecard, campaign brief, RACI, launch calendar, lead-sharing rules

Lead with the recommendation. Keep the main response to one executive page when practical; place working detail after it. End with **Decide now / Do next / Verify before use**.

## Human approval gate

Require consent, data-sharing terms, brand permissions, and an exit plan before activation.

External publishing, sending, spending, deleting, or system changes require explicit authorization.

## Score it with

- net-new reach, accepted leads, influenced pipeline, partner-sourced revenue, repeatability

## Quality bar

- Tie every recommendation to a customer, commercial, category, or capability decision.
- Separate verified facts, reasonable inferences, and open assumptions.
- Remove corporate oatmeal and unsupported precision.
- Name an owner, a next action, and the evidence that would change the decision.
