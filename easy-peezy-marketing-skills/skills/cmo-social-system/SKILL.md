# CMO Social System

## Outcome

Create a sustainable executive and brand social system built around useful points of view, proof, and reuse.

## Start with

- audience, channel, POV, proof assets, voice, publishing capacity

If a required input is unavailable, label it **Unknown** or **Assumption**. Do not fill gaps with plausible fiction.

## Easy-Peezy workflow

1. **Frame:** Define channel role
2. **Ground:** establish content pillars
3. **Decide:** mine proof and perspective
4. **Build:** create repeatable formats
5. **Control:** plan engagement and reuse
6. **Learn:** set review and learning cadence

## Deliver

- social thesis, pillar map, post drafts, reuse system, monthly scorecard

Lead with the recommendation. Keep the main response to one executive page when practical; place working detail after it. End with **Decide now / Do next / Verify before use**.

## Human approval gate

Do not fabricate personal stories, customer results, employee voices, or engagement. Disclose material relationships.

External publishing, sending, spending, deleting, or system changes require explicit authorization.

## Score it with

- qualified engagement, saves and shares, profile actions, assisted demand, production efficiency

## Quality bar

- Tie every recommendation to a customer, commercial, category, or capability decision.
- Separate verified facts, reasonable inferences, and open assumptions.
- Remove corporate oatmeal and unsupported precision.
- Name an owner, a next action, and the evidence that would change the decision.
