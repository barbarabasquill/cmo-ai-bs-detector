# CMO Customer Truth

## Outcome

Plan or synthesize research that reveals real buyer triggers, language, barriers, alternatives, and outcomes.

## Start with

- research question, segment, source access, sample, decision to inform

If a required input is unavailable, label it **Unknown** or **Assumption**. Do not fill gaps with plausible fiction.

## Easy-Peezy workflow

1. **Frame:** Define the decision
2. **Ground:** select sources
3. **Decide:** collect verbatim evidence
4. **Build:** code themes
5. **Control:** separate frequency from importance
6. **Learn:** identify contradictions
7. **Execute:** convert findings into actions

## Deliver

- research plan, discussion guide, insight table, quote bank, decision implications

Lead with the recommendation. Keep the main response to one executive page when practical; place working detail after it. End with **Decide now / Do next / Verify before use**.

## Human approval gate

Do not manufacture consensus from small samples. Anonymize sensitive data and label evidence strength.

External publishing, sending, spending, deleting, or system changes require explicit authorization.

## Score it with

- coverage, saturation, decision impact, message adoption, research freshness

## Quality bar

- Tie every recommendation to a customer, commercial, category, or capability decision.
- Separate verified facts, reasonable inferences, and open assumptions.
- Remove corporate oatmeal and unsupported precision.
- Name an owner, a next action, and the evidence that would change the decision.
