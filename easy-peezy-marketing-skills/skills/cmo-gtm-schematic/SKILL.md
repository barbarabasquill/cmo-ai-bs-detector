# CMO GTM Schematic

## Outcome

Turn a complex go-to-market problem into a one-page operating diagram with choices, motion, ownership, and metrics.

## Start with

- market trigger, ICP, buying group, promise, proof, demand motion, sales motion, timeline

If a required input is unavailable, label it **Unknown** or **Assumption**. Do not fill gaps with plausible fiction.

## Easy-Peezy workflow

1. **Frame:** Clarify the market change
2. **Ground:** define account and buying group
3. **Decide:** state differentiated promise
4. **Build:** map demand-to-revenue motion
5. **Control:** assign proof and owners
6. **Learn:** add 90-day activation and scorecard

## Deliver

- one-page schematic, assumption ledger, 90-day plan, CMO scorecard

Lead with the recommendation. Keep the main response to one executive page when practical; place working detail after it. End with **Decide now / Do next / Verify before use**.

## Human approval gate

An attractive diagram is not evidence. Label assumptions and expose missing buyer, proof, and capacity inputs.

External publishing, sending, spending, deleting, or system changes require explicit authorization.

## Score it with

- time to alignment, assumption closure, stage progression, owner accountability, launch readiness

## Quality bar

- Tie every recommendation to a customer, commercial, category, or capability decision.
- Separate verified facts, reasonable inferences, and open assumptions.
- Remove corporate oatmeal and unsupported precision.
- Name an owner, a next action, and the evidence that would change the decision.
