# CMO Search Health

## Outcome

Translate technical and content SEO findings into an executive priority list tied to discoverability and business value.

## Start with

- site, search data, analytics, technical access, business priorities, recent changes

If a required input is unavailable, label it **Unknown** or **Assumption**. Do not fill gaps with plausible fiction.

## Easy-Peezy workflow

1. **Frame:** Establish symptom and timing
2. **Ground:** check indexation and technical blockers
3. **Decide:** assess intent and content quality
4. **Build:** inspect authority and internal links
5. **Control:** prioritize by impact and evidence
6. **Learn:** assign owners

## Deliver

- executive audit, issue backlog, quick wins, dependency map, verification plan

Lead with the recommendation. Keep the main response to one executive page when practical; place working detail after it. End with **Decide now / Do next / Verify before use**.

## Human approval gate

Separate observed facts from hypotheses. Do not promise rankings or recommend bulk content without a quality case.

External publishing, sending, spending, deleting, or system changes require explicit authorization.

## Score it with

- indexation, qualified organic demand, conversion, issue closure, recovery trajectory

## Quality bar

- Tie every recommendation to a customer, commercial, category, or capability decision.
- Separate verified facts, reasonable inferences, and open assumptions.
- Remove corporate oatmeal and unsupported precision.
- Name an owner, a next action, and the evidence that would change the decision.
