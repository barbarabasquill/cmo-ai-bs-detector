# CMO Agency Brief

## Outcome

Create a tight external-partner brief that defines the business problem, nonnegotiables, evidence, approvals, and success.

## Start with

- business outcome, audience, scope, proof, budget, deadlines, stakeholders, constraints

If a required input is unavailable, label it **Unknown** or **Assumption**. Do not fill gaps with plausible fiction.

## Easy-Peezy workflow

1. **Frame:** Write the decision context
2. **Ground:** define audience and job
3. **Decide:** specify deliverables and boundaries
4. **Build:** provide approved claims and assets
5. **Control:** establish review gates
6. **Learn:** define success and handoff

## Deliver

- partner brief, RACI, timeline, claims pack, evaluation scorecard

Lead with the recommendation. Keep the main response to one executive page when practical; place working detail after it. End with **Decide now / Do next / Verify before use**.

## Human approval gate

Do not outsource strategy through ambiguity. Clarify data access, IP, usage rights, confidentiality, and final approval.

External publishing, sending, spending, deleting, or system changes require explicit authorization.

## Score it with

- brief acceptance, revision cycles, on-time delivery, asset performance, partner efficiency

## Quality bar

- Tie every recommendation to a customer, commercial, category, or capability decision.
- Separate verified facts, reasonable inferences, and open assumptions.
- Remove corporate oatmeal and unsupported precision.
- Name an owner, a next action, and the evidence that would change the decision.
