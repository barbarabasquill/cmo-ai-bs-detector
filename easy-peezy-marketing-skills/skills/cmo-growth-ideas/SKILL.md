# CMO Growth Ideas

## Outcome

Generate a short list of defensible growth ideas matched to the company’s stage, audience, evidence, and capacity.

## Start with

- goal, audience, constraints, existing strengths, proof, time horizon

If a required input is unavailable, label it **Unknown** or **Assumption**. Do not fill gaps with plausible fiction.

## Easy-Peezy workflow

1. **Frame:** Find the bottleneck
2. **Ground:** generate across mechanisms
3. **Decide:** remove duplicates and vanity plays
4. **Build:** score impact, confidence, effort, and fit
5. **Control:** select a balanced portfolio
6. **Learn:** define first test

## Deliver

- ranked idea portfolio, rationale, first experiments, dependency list, kill criteria

Lead with the recommendation. Keep the main response to one executive page when practical; place working detail after it. End with **Decide now / Do next / Verify before use**.

## Human approval gate

Do not substitute idea volume for strategy. Flag ideas dependent on unverified assumptions or unavailable capabilities.

External publishing, sending, spending, deleting, or system changes require explicit authorization.

## Score it with

- validated ideas, cycle time, incremental demand, resource efficiency, learning rate

## Quality bar

- Tie every recommendation to a customer, commercial, category, or capability decision.
- Separate verified facts, reasonable inferences, and open assumptions.
- Remove corporate oatmeal and unsupported precision.
- Name an owner, a next action, and the evidence that would change the decision.
