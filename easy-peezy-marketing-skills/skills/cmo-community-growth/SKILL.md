# CMO Community Growth

## Outcome

Design a community that delivers member value before demanding brand value.

## Start with

- member identity, recurring problem, host resources, platform, business role

If a required input is unavailable, label it **Unknown** or **Assumption**. Do not fill gaps with plausible fiction.

## Easy-Peezy workflow

1. **Frame:** Define the member promise
2. **Ground:** select rituals
3. **Decide:** recruit founding members
4. **Build:** establish moderation
5. **Control:** connect insights to the business
6. **Learn:** measure health before monetization

## Deliver

- community charter, 90-day plan, ritual calendar, moderation rules, health dashboard

Lead with the recommendation. Keep the main response to one executive page when practical; place working detail after it. End with **Decide now / Do next / Verify before use**.

## Human approval gate

Do not treat the community as an owned audience list. Protect consent, psychological safety, and member data.

External publishing, sending, spending, deleting, or system changes require explicit authorization.

## Score it with

- active members, contribution rate, return rate, member outcomes, assisted pipeline

## Quality bar

- Tie every recommendation to a customer, commercial, category, or capability decision.
- Separate verified facts, reasonable inferences, and open assumptions.
- Remove corporate oatmeal and unsupported precision.
- Name an owner, a next action, and the evidence that would change the decision.
