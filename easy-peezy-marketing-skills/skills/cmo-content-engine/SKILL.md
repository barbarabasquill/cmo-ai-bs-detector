# CMO Content Engine

## Outcome

Build a focused content system that turns customer questions and company proof into demand, trust, and sales utility.

## Start with

- business objective, audience questions, proof assets, distribution access, team capacity

If a required input is unavailable, label it **Unknown** or **Assumption**. Do not fill gaps with plausible fiction.

## Easy-Peezy workflow

1. **Frame:** Prioritize customer decisions
2. **Ground:** create pillar themes
3. **Decide:** map funnel and sales uses
4. **Build:** design formats and distribution
5. **Control:** set reuse rules
6. **Learn:** establish kill and refresh criteria

## Deliver

- content thesis, pillar map, 90-day calendar, distribution plan, measurement model

Lead with the recommendation. Keep the main response to one executive page when practical; place working detail after it. End with **Decide now / Do next / Verify before use**.

## Human approval gate

Do not fill a calendar with volume for its own sake. Require a distinct audience, decision, and distribution path.

External publishing, sending, spending, deleting, or system changes require explicit authorization.

## Score it with

- qualified consumption, return audience, sales use, assisted pipeline, content efficiency

## Quality bar

- Tie every recommendation to a customer, commercial, category, or capability decision.
- Separate verified facts, reasonable inferences, and open assumptions.
- Remove corporate oatmeal and unsupported precision.
- Name an owner, a next action, and the evidence that would change the decision.
