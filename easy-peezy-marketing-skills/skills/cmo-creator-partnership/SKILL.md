# CMO Creator Partnership

## Outcome

Build transparent creator partnerships around audience fit, credible proof, and measurable business value.

## Start with

- audience, creator candidates, offer, budget, claims, disclosure rules

If a required input is unavailable, label it **Unknown** or **Assumption**. Do not fill gaps with plausible fiction.

## Easy-Peezy workflow

1. **Frame:** Define the partnership job
2. **Ground:** score audience and credibility fit
3. **Decide:** choose compensation and rights
4. **Build:** brief authentic content
5. **Control:** require disclosures
6. **Learn:** measure quality beyond reach

## Deliver

- creator scorecard, outreach brief, contract checklist, content brief, results dashboard

Lead with the recommendation. Keep the main response to one executive page when practical; place working detail after it. End with **Decide now / Do next / Verify before use**.

## Human approval gate

FTC-compliant disclosure is mandatory. Never script false experiences or hide payment and material connections.

External publishing, sending, spending, deleting, or system changes require explicit authorization.

## Score it with

- qualified reach, content saves, attributed demand, usable assets, brand-safety incidents

## Quality bar

- Tie every recommendation to a customer, commercial, category, or capability decision.
- Separate verified facts, reasonable inferences, and open assumptions.
- Remove corporate oatmeal and unsupported precision.
- Name an owner, a next action, and the evidence that would change the decision.
