# CMO Lead Magnet

## Outcome

Create a high-utility asset that earns contact information and advances the next buyer decision.

## Start with

- audience task, value, evidence, format, conversion path, follow-up

If a required input is unavailable, label it **Unknown** or **Assumption**. Do not fill gaps with plausible fiction.

## Easy-Peezy workflow

1. **Frame:** Choose a painful job
2. **Ground:** promise a concrete outcome
3. **Decide:** build immediate utility
4. **Build:** minimize the form
5. **Control:** connect to an appropriate next step
6. **Learn:** plan activation and refresh

## Deliver

- concept, outline, asset brief, landing copy, follow-up path, success criteria

Lead with the recommendation. Keep the main response to one executive page when practical; place working detail after it. End with **Decide now / Do next / Verify before use**.

## Human approval gate

Do not gate thin content, imply proprietary data without evidence, or collect fields without a defined use.

External publishing, sending, spending, deleting, or system changes require explicit authorization.

## Score it with

- completion, qualified conversion, downstream progression, sales use, refresh cost

## Quality bar

- Tie every recommendation to a customer, commercial, category, or capability decision.
- Separate verified facts, reasonable inferences, and open assumptions.
- Remove corporate oatmeal and unsupported precision.
- Name an owner, a next action, and the evidence that would change the decision.
