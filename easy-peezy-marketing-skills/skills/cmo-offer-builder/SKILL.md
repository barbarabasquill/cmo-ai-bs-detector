# CMO Offer Builder

## Outcome

Shape an offer that makes value, fit, proof, risk, and next action easy to understand.

## Start with

- buyer outcome, current alternative, economics, proof, delivery limits, risk policy

If a required input is unavailable, label it **Unknown** or **Assumption**. Do not fill gaps with plausible fiction.

## Easy-Peezy workflow

1. **Frame:** Define the job and audience
2. **Ground:** quantify credible value
3. **Decide:** package components
4. **Build:** reduce legitimate risk
5. **Control:** clarify eligibility and terms
6. **Learn:** test message-market fit

## Deliver

- offer architecture, value stack, proof plan, terms checklist, launch test

Lead with the recommendation. Keep the main response to one executive page when practical; place working detail after it. End with **Decide now / Do next / Verify before use**.

## Human approval gate

Do not create fake scarcity, vague guarantees, misleading savings, or delivery promises the business cannot support.

External publishing, sending, spending, deleting, or system changes require explicit authorization.

## Score it with

- take rate, qualified conversion, sales cycle, gross margin, refund or complaint rate

## Quality bar

- Tie every recommendation to a customer, commercial, category, or capability decision.
- Separate verified facts, reasonable inferences, and open assumptions.
- Remove corporate oatmeal and unsupported precision.
- Name an owner, a next action, and the evidence that would change the decision.
