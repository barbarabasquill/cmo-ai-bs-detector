# CMO Decision Council

## Outcome

Simulate a disciplined cross-functional challenge session without pretending fictional experts are real authorities.

## Start with

- decision, options, evidence, constraints, stakeholders, deadline

If a required input is unavailable, label it **Unknown** or **Assumption**. Do not fill gaps with plausible fiction.

## Easy-Peezy workflow

1. **Frame:** Frame the decision
2. **Ground:** assign functional lenses
3. **Decide:** present strongest case for each option
4. **Build:** surface conflicts and missing evidence
5. **Control:** run a pre-mortem
6. **Learn:** recommend with dissent

## Deliver

- council memo, option table, risk register, dissent note, decision request

Lead with the recommendation. Keep the main response to one executive page when practical; place working detail after it. End with **Decide now / Do next / Verify before use**.

## Human approval gate

Label the council as simulated analysis. Do not fabricate quotes, endorsements, or named expert opinions.

External publishing, sending, spending, deleting, or system changes require explicit authorization.

## Score it with

- decision speed, risk closure, stakeholder alignment, assumption validation, outcome review

## Quality bar

- Tie every recommendation to a customer, commercial, category, or capability decision.
- Separate verified facts, reasonable inferences, and open assumptions.
- Remove corporate oatmeal and unsupported precision.
- Name an owner, a next action, and the evidence that would change the decision.
