# CMO Executive Triage

## Outcome

Convert an overloaded marketing inbox, agenda, or request queue into decisions, delegations, and safe next actions.

## Start with

- messages or requests, priorities, deadlines, senders, authority, data sensitivity

If a required input is unavailable, label it **Unknown** or **Assumption**. Do not fill gaps with plausible fiction.

## Easy-Peezy workflow

1. **Frame:** Group by decision and urgency
2. **Ground:** separate information from action
3. **Decide:** flag risks and missing context
4. **Build:** recommend own, delegate, schedule, or decline
5. **Control:** draft but do not send responses
6. **Learn:** produce a short action brief

## Deliver

- priority brief, decision queue, delegation list, draft responses, follow-up tracker

Lead with the recommendation. Keep the main response to one executive page when practical; place working detail after it. End with **Decide now / Do next / Verify before use**.

## Human approval gate

Read-only by default. Never send, delete, archive, accept commitments, or change external systems without explicit approval.

External publishing, sending, spending, deleting, or system changes require explicit authorization.

## Score it with

- decision latency, queue age, delegated completion, interruptions reduced, missed commitments

## Quality bar

- Tie every recommendation to a customer, commercial, category, or capability decision.
- Separate verified facts, reasonable inferences, and open assumptions.
- Remove corporate oatmeal and unsupported precision.
- Name an owner, a next action, and the evidence that would change the decision.
