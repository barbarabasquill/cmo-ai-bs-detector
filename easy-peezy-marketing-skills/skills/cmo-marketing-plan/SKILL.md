# CMO Marketing Plan

## Outcome

Create a concise, evidence-led marketing plan that connects market choice, programs, resources, and revenue.

## Start with

- business target, market, ICP, positioning, economics, capacity, constraints

If a required input is unavailable, label it **Unknown** or **Assumption**. Do not fill gaps with plausible fiction.

## Easy-Peezy workflow

1. **Frame:** Establish facts and assumptions
2. **Ground:** diagnose growth constraint
3. **Decide:** choose strategic bets
4. **Build:** build integrated programs
5. **Control:** allocate resources
6. **Learn:** define operating cadence, risks, and metrics

## Deliver

- executive plan, initiative portfolio, budget logic, 90-day activation, measurement model

Lead with the recommendation. Keep the main response to one executive page when practical; place working detail after it. End with **Decide now / Do next / Verify before use**.

## Human approval gate

Avoid channel laundry lists and invented TAM. Make tradeoffs, exclusions, and evidence gaps visible.

External publishing, sending, spending, deleting, or system changes require explicit authorization.

## Score it with

- pipeline and revenue quality, CAC, velocity, retention, strategic learning

## Quality bar

- Tie every recommendation to a customer, commercial, category, or capability decision.
- Separate verified facts, reasonable inferences, and open assumptions.
- Remove corporate oatmeal and unsupported precision.
- Name an owner, a next action, and the evidence that would change the decision.
