# CMO Ad Creative Brief

## Outcome

Create differentiated paid-media concepts that a creative team can produce without guessing.

## Start with

- audience tension, offer, proof, channel, format, brand limits

If a required input is unavailable, label it **Unknown** or **Assumption**. Do not fill gaps with plausible fiction.

## Easy-Peezy workflow

1. **Frame:** Mine buyer language
2. **Ground:** define three distinct angles
3. **Decide:** pair each angle with hook, proof, visual idea, and CTA
4. **Build:** plan variants
5. **Control:** set review and fatigue rules

## Deliver

- creative strategy, concept cards, production brief, approval checklist

Lead with the recommendation. Keep the main response to one executive page when practical; place working detail after it. End with **Decide now / Do next / Verify before use**.

## Human approval gate

Never invent testimonials, benchmarks, awards, or product capabilities. Label dramatizations and required disclosures.

External publishing, sending, spending, deleting, or system changes require explicit authorization.

## Score it with

- thumb-stop rate, CTR, qualified conversion, cost per opportunity, creative fatigue

## Quality bar

- Tie every recommendation to a customer, commercial, category, or capability decision.
- Separate verified facts, reasonable inferences, and open assumptions.
- Remove corporate oatmeal and unsupported precision.
- Name an owner, a next action, and the evidence that would change the decision.
