# CMO Copy Cleanup

## Outcome

Edit marketing copy for clarity, specificity, evidence, voice, and action while preserving the intended meaning.

## Start with

- original copy, audience, objective, voice rules, required claims

If a required input is unavailable, label it **Unknown** or **Assumption**. Do not fill gaps with plausible fiction.

## Easy-Peezy workflow

1. **Frame:** Diagnose before rewriting
2. **Ground:** remove corporate oatmeal
3. **Decide:** tighten structure
4. **Build:** replace vague claims with supported specifics
5. **Control:** preserve facts
6. **Learn:** show material edits and unresolved questions

## Deliver

- clean draft, change rationale, unsupported-claim list, final approval checklist

Lead with the recommendation. Keep the main response to one executive page when practical; place working detail after it. End with **Decide now / Do next / Verify before use**.

## Human approval gate

Do not silently change factual meaning, legal language, pricing, or commitments. Flag rather than guess.

External publishing, sending, spending, deleting, or system changes require explicit authorization.

## Score it with

- clarity, message recall, conversion signal, review cycles, claim accuracy

## Quality bar

- Tie every recommendation to a customer, commercial, category, or capability decision.
- Separate verified facts, reasonable inferences, and open assumptions.
- Remove corporate oatmeal and unsupported precision.
- Name an owner, a next action, and the evidence that would change the decision.
