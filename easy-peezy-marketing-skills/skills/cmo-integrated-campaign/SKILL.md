# CMO Integrated Campaign

## Outcome

Build an integrated campaign around one audience change, one commercial outcome, and coordinated channel roles.

## Start with

- business goal, audience, insight, offer, proof, regions, channels, sales capacity

If a required input is unavailable, label it **Unknown** or **Assumption**. Do not fill gaps with plausible fiction.

## Easy-Peezy workflow

1. **Frame:** Define the buyer change
2. **Ground:** create message and offer spine
3. **Decide:** assign channel jobs
4. **Build:** sequence awareness through conversion
5. **Control:** align sales and partners
6. **Learn:** set measurement and optimization cadence

## Deliver

- campaign brief, journey map, channel plan, content matrix, scorecard, RACI

Lead with the recommendation. Keep the main response to one executive page when practical; place working detail after it. End with **Decide now / Do next / Verify before use**.

## Human approval gate

Do not call a bundle of disconnected tactics integrated. Require a shared audience, promise, offer, and measurement logic.

External publishing, sending, spending, deleting, or system changes require explicit authorization.

## Score it with

- target-account engagement, progression, accepted demand, pipeline, content utilization

## Quality bar

- Tie every recommendation to a customer, commercial, category, or capability decision.
- Separate verified facts, reasonable inferences, and open assumptions.
- Remove corporate oatmeal and unsupported precision.
- Name an owner, a next action, and the evidence that would change the decision.
