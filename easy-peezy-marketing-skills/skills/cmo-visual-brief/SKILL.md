# CMO Visual Brief

## Outcome

Turn a marketing objective into a production-ready visual brief with brand, channel, and accuracy controls.

## Start with

- objective, audience, placement, dimensions, brand rules, must-show and must-avoid

If a required input is unavailable, label it **Unknown** or **Assumption**. Do not fill gaps with plausible fiction.

## Easy-Peezy workflow

1. **Frame:** Choose the communication job
2. **Ground:** define composition and hierarchy
3. **Decide:** specify copy and safe zones
4. **Build:** write generation or design direction
5. **Control:** plan variants
6. **Learn:** run accessibility and claims QA

## Deliver

- visual brief, generation prompt, art direction, variant plan, QA checklist

Lead with the recommendation. Keep the main response to one executive page when practical; place working detail after it. End with **Decide now / Do next / Verify before use**.

## Human approval gate

Avoid deceptive product imagery, unauthorized likenesses, trademark misuse, and inaccessible text treatment.

External publishing, sending, spending, deleting, or system changes require explicit authorization.

## Score it with

- message recognition, engagement, qualified action, production speed, reuse rate

## Quality bar

- Tie every recommendation to a customer, commercial, category, or capability decision.
- Separate verified facts, reasonable inferences, and open assumptions.
- Remove corporate oatmeal and unsupported precision.
- Name an owner, a next action, and the evidence that would change the decision.
