# CMO Retention Rescue

## Outcome

Diagnose churn and design retention interventions by cause, segment, and moment.

## Start with

- cohort retention, reasons, product usage, customer value, renewal process

If a required input is unavailable, label it **Unknown** or **Assumption**. Do not fill gaps with plausible fiction.

## Easy-Peezy workflow

1. **Frame:** Segment voluntary and involuntary churn
2. **Ground:** quantify causes
3. **Decide:** identify leading signals
4. **Build:** design interventions
5. **Control:** set ethical save rules
6. **Learn:** measure cohort impact

## Deliver

- churn tree, intervention matrix, journey copy, experiment plan, owner map

Lead with the recommendation. Keep the main response to one executive page when practical; place working detail after it. End with **Decide now / Do next / Verify before use**.

## Human approval gate

Do not dark-pattern cancellation or conceal terms. Preserve easy cancellation and customer choice.

External publishing, sending, spending, deleting, or system changes require explicit authorization.

## Score it with

- gross retention, net retention, save rate, reactivation, complaint rate

## Quality bar

- Tie every recommendation to a customer, commercial, category, or capability decision.
- Separate verified facts, reasonable inferences, and open assumptions.
- Remove corporate oatmeal and unsupported precision.
- Name an owner, a next action, and the evidence that would change the decision.
