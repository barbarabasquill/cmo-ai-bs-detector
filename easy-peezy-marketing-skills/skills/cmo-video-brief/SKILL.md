# CMO Video Brief

## Outcome

Turn a marketing objective into a production-ready video concept with narrative, evidence, format, and distribution.

## Start with

- audience, objective, channel, duration, proof, speakers, production limits

If a required input is unavailable, label it **Unknown** or **Assumption**. Do not fill gaps with plausible fiction.

## Easy-Peezy workflow

1. **Frame:** Choose one viewer change
2. **Ground:** define hook and narrative
3. **Decide:** assign proof and visuals
4. **Build:** write script beats
5. **Control:** design platform variants and CTA
6. **Learn:** run rights and claims review

## Deliver

- creative brief, storyboard, script, shot list, variant plan, QA checklist

Lead with the recommendation. Keep the main response to one executive page when practical; place working detail after it. End with **Decide now / Do next / Verify before use**.

## Human approval gate

Do not clone voices or likenesses without permission, fabricate demonstrations, or hide synthetic-media disclosures when required.

External publishing, sending, spending, deleting, or system changes require explicit authorization.

## Score it with

- hold rate, completion, qualified action, reuse, production cycle time

## Quality bar

- Tie every recommendation to a customer, commercial, category, or capability decision.
- Separate verified facts, reasonable inferences, and open assumptions.
- Remove corporate oatmeal and unsupported precision.
- Name an owner, a next action, and the evidence that would change the decision.
