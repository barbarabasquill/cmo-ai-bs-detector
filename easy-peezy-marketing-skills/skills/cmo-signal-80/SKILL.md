# CMO Signal 80

## Outcome

Audit executive attention and shift it toward customer truth, revenue quality, category position, and strategic execution.

## Start with

- calendar, active projects, meeting purpose, decisions, delegations, future bets

If a required input is unavailable, label it **Unknown** or **Assumption**. Do not fill gaps with plausible fiction.

## Easy-Peezy workflow

1. **Frame:** Classify work into four growth signals, future bets, or noise
2. **Ground:** calculate attention ratio
3. **Decide:** identify own, delegate, automate, and stop actions
4. **Build:** choose three next-week decisions

## Deliver

- attention audit, Signal 80 score, stop list, delegation plan, next-week priorities

Lead with the recommendation. Keep the main response to one executive page when practical; place working detail after it. End with **Decide now / Do next / Verify before use**.

## Human approval gate

Do not label unavoidable governance or people care as noise merely because it lacks immediate revenue.

External publishing, sending, spending, deleting, or system changes require explicit authorization.

## Score it with

- signal ratio, decisions closed, hours reclaimed, delegated work, strategic throughput

## Quality bar

- Tie every recommendation to a customer, commercial, category, or capability decision.
- Separate verified facts, reasonable inferences, and open assumptions.
- Remove corporate oatmeal and unsupported precision.
- Name an owner, a next action, and the evidence that would change the decision.
