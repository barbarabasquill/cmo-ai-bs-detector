# CMO Account Prospecting

## Outcome

Define and build a high-fit prospecting approach using verifiable firmographic, technographic, and trigger evidence.

## Start with

- ICP, exclusions, signals, geography, data sources, outreach capacity

If a required input is unavailable, label it **Unknown** or **Assumption**. Do not fill gaps with plausible fiction.

## Easy-Peezy workflow

1. **Frame:** Translate ICP into observable criteria
2. **Ground:** define triggers and disqualifiers
3. **Decide:** prioritize accounts
4. **Build:** verify contacts
5. **Control:** record evidence
6. **Learn:** route to the right motion

## Deliver

- account criteria, scoring model, research template, prioritized list specification, QA rules

Lead with the recommendation. Keep the main response to one executive page when practical; place working detail after it. End with **Decide now / Do next / Verify before use**.

## Human approval gate

Use lawful, authorized data. Do not infer sensitive traits or fabricate roles, emails, or triggers.

External publishing, sending, spending, deleting, or system changes require explicit authorization.

## Score it with

- fit rate, contact validity, positive reply, opportunity creation, data decay

## Quality bar

- Tie every recommendation to a customer, commercial, category, or capability decision.
- Separate verified facts, reasonable inferences, and open assumptions.
- Remove corporate oatmeal and unsupported precision.
- Name an owner, a next action, and the evidence that would change the decision.
