# CMO Experiment Call

## Outcome

Turn a marketing question into a decision-ready experiment with a clear hypothesis, guardrails, owner, and stop rule.

## Start with

- baseline, audience, primary outcome, decision threshold, test window

If a required input is unavailable, label it **Unknown** or **Assumption**. Do not fill gaps with plausible fiction.

## Easy-Peezy workflow

1. **Frame:** Separate the business decision from the test tactic
2. **Ground:** define one primary metric
3. **Decide:** check sample and instrumentation
4. **Build:** prewrite win, lose, and inconclusive actions
5. **Control:** assign an owner and readout date

## Deliver

- experiment card, measurement table, risk notes, executive recommendation

Lead with the recommendation. Keep the main response to one executive page when practical; place working detail after it. End with **Decide now / Do next / Verify before use**.

## Human approval gate

Do not call a directional result statistically proven. Flag overlapping tests, tiny samples, novelty effects, and broken tracking.

External publishing, sending, spending, deleting, or system changes require explicit authorization.

## Score it with

- incremental conversion, qualified pipeline, revenue per visitor, decision cycle time

## Quality bar

- Tie every recommendation to a customer, commercial, category, or capability decision.
- Separate verified facts, reasonable inferences, and open assumptions.
- Remove corporate oatmeal and unsupported precision.
- Name an owner, a next action, and the evidence that would change the decision.
