# Easy-Peezy CMO Capability Map

Use this map when the business question is clear but the right skill is not.

## Executive operating system

- [CMO Board Narrative](../skills/cmo-board-narrative/SKILL.md) — Turn marketing performance into a concise board story about growth, risk, decisions, and next actions.
- [CMO Budget Allocator](../skills/cmo-budget-allocator/SKILL.md) — Allocate marketing resources across core growth, optimization, experimentation, and resilience using explicit assumptions.
- [CMO Decision Council](../skills/cmo-decision-council/SKILL.md) — Simulate a disciplined cross-functional challenge session without pretending fictional experts are real authorities.
- [CMO Executive Triage](../skills/cmo-executive-triage/SKILL.md) — Convert an overloaded marketing inbox, agenda, or request queue into decisions, delegations, and safe next actions.
- [CMO GTM Schematic](../skills/cmo-gtm-schematic/SKILL.md) — Turn a complex go-to-market problem into a one-page operating diagram with choices, motion, ownership, and metrics.
- [CMO Signal 80](../skills/cmo-signal-80/SKILL.md) — Audit executive attention and shift it toward customer truth, revenue quality, category position, and strategic execution.

## Strategy and market

- [CMO Market Foundation](../skills/cmo-market-foundation/SKILL.md) — Create the shared market context that every other Easy-Peezy skill uses: audience, problem, promise, proof, objections, and constraints.
- [CMO Marketing Plan](../skills/cmo-marketing-plan/SKILL.md) — Create a concise, evidence-led marketing plan that connects market choice, programs, resources, and revenue.
- [CMO Customer Truth](../skills/cmo-customer-truth/SKILL.md) — Plan or synthesize research that reveals real buyer triggers, language, barriers, alternatives, and outcomes.
- [CMO Competitor Dossier](../skills/cmo-competitor-dossier/SKILL.md) — Build a sourced competitor profile focused on choices, proof, buyer implications, and likely moves.
- [CMO Pricing Decision](../skills/cmo-pricing-decision/SKILL.md) — Structure a pricing decision using customer value, willingness to pay, competitive context, economics, and migration risk.
- [CMO Offer Builder](../skills/cmo-offer-builder/SKILL.md) — Shape an offer that makes value, fit, proof, risk, and next action easy to understand.

## Demand and campaigns

- [CMO ABM Command](../skills/cmo-abm-command/SKILL.md) — Prioritize accounts, buying groups, plays, and orchestration for a focused account-based growth motion.
- [CMO Integrated Campaign](../skills/cmo-integrated-campaign/SKILL.md) — Build an integrated campaign around one audience change, one commercial outcome, and coordinated channel roles.
- [CMO Paid Media Command](../skills/cmo-paid-media-command/SKILL.md) — Build or audit a paid-media plan around business economics instead of platform activity.
- [CMO Event Pipeline](../skills/cmo-event-pipeline/SKILL.md) — Build an event strategy that connects audience, experience, follow-up, and measurable commercial outcomes.
- [CMO Partner Campaign](../skills/cmo-partner-campaign/SKILL.md) — Turn a possible partnership into a balanced campaign with shared audience, value, work, and measurement.
- [CMO Launch Command](../skills/cmo-launch-command/SKILL.md) — Orchestrate a launch around market readiness, buyer proof, internal alignment, and post-launch learning.

## Content and creative

- [CMO Content Engine](../skills/cmo-content-engine/SKILL.md) — Build a focused content system that turns customer questions and company proof into demand, trust, and sales utility.
- [CMO Copy Studio](../skills/cmo-copy-studio/SKILL.md) — Draft decision-focused marketing copy using buyer language, credible proof, and a clear next action.
- [CMO Copy Cleanup](../skills/cmo-copy-cleanup/SKILL.md) — Edit marketing copy for clarity, specificity, evidence, voice, and action while preserving the intended meaning.
- [CMO Ad Creative Brief](../skills/cmo-ad-creative-brief/SKILL.md) — Create differentiated paid-media concepts that a creative team can produce without guessing.
- [CMO Visual Brief](../skills/cmo-visual-brief/SKILL.md) — Turn a marketing objective into a production-ready visual brief with brand, channel, and accuracy controls.
- [CMO Video Brief](../skills/cmo-video-brief/SKILL.md) — Turn a marketing objective into a production-ready video concept with narrative, evidence, format, and distribution.
- [CMO Social System](../skills/cmo-social-system/SKILL.md) — Create a sustainable executive and brand social system built around useful points of view, proof, and reuse.

## Revenue and lifecycle

- [CMO Pipeline Diagnostic](../skills/cmo-pipeline-diagnostic/SKILL.md) — Find where pipeline quality or velocity is breaking and identify the smallest set of corrective actions.
- [CMO Revenue Handoff](../skills/cmo-revenue-handoff/SKILL.md) — Design the lifecycle, definitions, routing, service levels, and feedback that connect marketing work to revenue.
- [CMO Sales Enablement](../skills/cmo-sales-enablement/SKILL.md) — Create sales assets and plays that help sellers make buyer conversations more relevant, credible, and consistent.
- [CMO Lifecycle Email](../skills/cmo-lifecycle-email/SKILL.md) — Design an email journey that advances a specific customer decision instead of sending disconnected messages.
- [CMO Activation Journey](../skills/cmo-activation-journey/SKILL.md) — Design the path from signup to first meaningful value with clear ownership and behavioral evidence.
- [CMO Retention Rescue](../skills/cmo-retention-rescue/SKILL.md) — Diagnose churn and design retention interventions by cause, segment, and moment.
- [CMO Customer Expansion](../skills/cmo-customer-expansion/SKILL.md) — Design customer marketing that increases adoption, advocacy, renewal confidence, and expansion without treating customers as leads.

## Digital growth

- [CMO Search Health](../skills/cmo-search-health/SKILL.md) — Translate technical and content SEO findings into an executive priority list tied to discoverability and business value.
- [CMO Answer-Engine Visibility](../skills/cmo-answer-engine-visibility/SKILL.md) — Improve how a brand is discovered, cited, and represented in AI-generated answers without making unsupported ranking promises.
- [CMO Scaled Search Pages](../skills/cmo-scaled-search-pages/SKILL.md) — Evaluate and plan template-driven search pages that remain genuinely useful at scale.
- [CMO Conversion Rescue](../skills/cmo-conversion-rescue/SKILL.md) — Diagnose why a page or form is losing qualified buyers and prioritize fixes by evidence and impact.
- [CMO Signup Rescue](../skills/cmo-signup-rescue/SKILL.md) — Reduce unnecessary signup friction while protecting qualification, trust, security, and downstream success.
- [CMO Website Map](../skills/cmo-website-map/SKILL.md) — Plan a website structure that reflects buyer decisions, search intent, product complexity, and internal ownership.

## AI governance

- [CMO AI BS Audit](../skills/cmo-ai-bs-audit/SKILL.md) — Review AI-generated marketing for unsupported claims, corporate oatmeal, fake insight, yes-man behavior, and risk.
- [CMO LLM Security Gate](../skills/cmo-llm-security-gate/SKILL.md) — Decide whether information and workflows are appropriate for an approved LLM before any content is entered or automated.
- [CMO Repeatable Growth Loop](../skills/cmo-repeatable-growth-loop/SKILL.md) — Design a recurring marketing workflow with a trigger, state, human checkpoint, and stop condition.
